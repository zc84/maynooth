ERC-BROKENSONG

A history
assembled from
fragments.

BROKENSONG studies how polyphonic music was written, shared and reshaped in medieval Britain and Ireland, c. 1150–1350.

Research questions

Music is made
between us.

Manuscripts preserve traces, not transparent recordings. The project combines musical analysis, manuscript studies, digital methods and performance-aware research to recover the social work carried out by musical notation.

♪
01 / MATERIAL TRACES

Recover what survives.

Read the relationships.
